**Prison Break: A Thrilling Escape Drama**
**Prison Break is an American television series that captivated audiences with its intense storyline and complex characters.**

**Overview**
"The prison is just the beginning."

The show premiered in 2005 and ran for five seasons, becoming one of the most popular thriller series of its time. Created by Paul Scheuring, it follows the story of two brothers whose lives become intertwined with conspiracy, loyalty, and survival.

**Main Characters**
**Character**            **Actor**                    **Role**

**Plot Summary**
